The sky box is both the scene's background and its environment lighting, so reflections match
what you see. It's built from layers: a colour, a panorama or a cube map at the bottom, then a
physically based atmosphere, suns and moons that light the scene, stars, nebulae, clouds and a
ground. A day-night cycle moves them all from a time of day.

::: scene exampleSkyBox
The `DAY_NIGHT` preset on a two-minute day, with keys to set the time and the speed. The
[sky box example](hub:examples/sky-box) walks through it.
:::
