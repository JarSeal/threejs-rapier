::: scene exampleLod
A field of 180 torus knots of 16,384 triangles each. The near ones are the knot itself, farther
ones are simplified copies, and the farthest are impostors: a single quad each.
:::

Drawn whole, the field would be about three million triangles. With levels of detail it's about
half a million from the start camera, in five instanced meshes: one per level.
